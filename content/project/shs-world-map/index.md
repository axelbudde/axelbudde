**SHS World Map** is an interactive R Shiny dashboard visualising [Serious Health-Related Suffering (SHS)](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(17)32513-8/fulltext) burden data across 195 countries, developed for the **Lancet Commission on Palliative Care and Pain Relief**.

SHS is a patient-centered epidemiological endpoint operationalising unmet need for palliative care — a direct application of outcome measurement methodology at population scale.

### Features

- Interactive choropleth maps with country-level SHS burden estimates
- Time series charts and hierarchical visualisations of health-burden indicators
- Responsive web dashboard with complex filtering for research and advocacy audiences
- Used in regulatory, policy, and clinical research contexts worldwide
